        🛡️ Trust, Verification & Security

        How we verify credibility

        Every beneficiary, project, and partner organization goes through an admin review before it's published — nothing appears publicly until HIH staff approve it. Beneficiaries can be biometrically identified (fingerprint via WebAuthn) and their support delivery is confirmed with photo/video, GPS location, and re-confirmed contact details. Partner organizations seeking funding submit verification documents — NGO statute, government registration certificate, recent bank statements, and an audit report if available (not mandatory for grassroots organizations still starting out) — reviewed by our team before their listing goes live. Donors can also become a Verified Donor through a one-time approval process, unlocking full case detail while keeping sensitive information away from the open internet.

        How we protect data

        All traffic to and from this app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, which encrypts all data at rest by default. Sensitive fields — photos, videos, GPS coordinates, phone numbers, financial account details, and verification documents — are stored in access-controlled records that are enforced server-side: only admins and Verified Donors can read them, regardless of what the app's interface shows. This is enforced by Firestore Security Rules, not just hidden in the app's design. Only pre-authorized admin accounts can approve, reject, or delete records.

        Where we're piloting

        HiHub is being alpha-tested with communities and partner networks in Burundi, starting in Bujumbura, with planned expansion across the Great Lakes region (Burundi, DRC, Rwanda) as the platform is validated. Beyond this initial pilot, we have already planned a broader expansion across the whole of Africa as the platform matures and partnerships grow.

        Questions about our verification process or security model? Reach out via the Contact Us form in the Support tab.
