        📄 Privacy Policy

        Last updated: 2026

        What we collect

        Depending on how you use HiHub, we may collect: your name, phone/email contact, physical location or GPS coordinates, photos or videos submitted for verification, fingerprint biometric data (processed locally on your device via WebAuthn — we never receive or store your raw fingerprint, only a cryptographic credential ID), financial account details you choose to provide for receiving support, and organizational verification documents (statute, government registration, audit reports, bank statements) submitted by partner organizations.

        How we use it

        Data is used to verify the legitimacy of beneficiaries, projects, and organizations; to coordinate and confirm delivery of donor support; to communicate with you about your submission; and to generate aggregate, non-identifying statistics for reporting to funders and partners. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

        Who can see it

        Basic case information (name, sector, general location, status) is visible to the public so donors can discover verified need. Sensitive details — photos, videos, exact GPS, contact numbers, financial details, and verification documents — are restricted to HIH admins and approved Verified Donors, enforced by our database security rules (see our Trust & Security page for detail).

        How long we keep it

        We retain records for as long as needed to maintain program history and satisfy funder reporting requirements, unless you request deletion sooner (see below).

        Third parties

        Data is stored using Google Firebase/Firestore. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We may share aggregate or case-specific information with a specific donor when it's necessary to coordinate the support you're requesting or providing.

        Your rights

        You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or request deletion, by reaching out via the Contact Us form in the Support tab or emailing info@holisticimpacthub.org.
